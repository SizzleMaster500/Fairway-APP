Round differentials

How Fairway estimates HCP

This is a personal playing-level indicator, not an official WHS Handicap Index. Each rated 18-hole round uses (gross strokes − Course Rating) × 113 / Slope. For 9-hole rounds we double that nine-hole differential as a simple approximation, NOT the official WHS expected-score calculation. We use a WHS-inspired best-differentials selection over the latest 20 rated rounds, but do not apply net double bogey, PCC, exceptional-score adjustments, soft/hard caps, or other official requirements. Unrated rounds remain in History and Awards but cannot produce a differential. Negative results may occur for exceptionally good rounds.
