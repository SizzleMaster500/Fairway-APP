How is this calculated?

Personal playing-level estimate from all saved 9- and 18-hole rounds with valid ratings. For 9 holes, Fairway doubles the played-nine differential as a transparent approximation; this is NOT the WHS expected-score method. For 18 holes, net double bogey is applied only when complete Stroke Index data and an official reference HI are available. Nine-hole results remain approximate; exceptional scores, caps and other WHS rules are not applied. Not an official Handicap Index.

Round differentials

How Fairway estimates HCP

This is a personal playing-level indicator, not an official WHS Handicap Index. Each rated 18-hole round uses (adjusted gross score − Course Rating − PCC) × 113 / Slope. Adjustment requires a complete Stroke Index and your entered official HI; otherwise gross score is used. For 9-hole rounds we double that nine-hole differential as a simple approximation, NOT the official WHS expected-score calculation. We use a WHS-inspired best-differentials selection over the latest 20 rated rounds, but do not automatically determine PCC, exceptional-score adjustments, soft/hard caps, or other official requirements. An entered official HI is only a reference, not a reconstructed historical HI. Unrated rounds remain in History and Awards but cannot produce a differential. Negative results may occur for exceptionally good rounds.
